Choose any of 193 countries with real population & GDP. Rule month by month: taxes, budgets, diplomacy, trade, alliances, sanctions — and war. Survive elections, crises & coups. 2D interactive world map. Made for phone & tablet 📱

🤖 How to play: 1) Pick nation 2) Tap map countries for diplomacy 3) Set taxes/budgets 4) Press Next Month. Install on Android via browser menu → “Add to Home screen” or build APK with Capacitor (see ANDROID.md).
